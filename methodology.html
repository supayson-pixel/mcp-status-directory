Methodology

Every score is computed only from public, verifiable signals. Nothing is estimated; unknown values are shown as “unchecked” and score zero.

Labels

Healthy ≥75 · Stale 40–74 · Broken <40 · Archived whenever GitHub marks the repo archived, regardless of score.

Known limits

  • Packages are verified for 48 of 93 servers. Monorepos, Go/Docker-only servers and repos without a root package.json / pyproject.toml stay “unchecked”, which caps them at 80.
  • Live MCP handshake tests are on the roadmap.
  • Maintainer notes (e.g. “no longer maintained” in a README) are flagged manually.
